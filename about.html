// ABOUT ATLAS CORPORATION

We are building
India's defense
operating system.

A two-person founding team from Kolkata, building the software and hardware infrastructure that India's borders will run on.

// MISSION

India's defense perimeter is monitored by fragmented, isolated systems that cannot talk to each other, cannot respond autonomously, and cannot scale. We are building Atlas OS — the unified intelligence layer that connects every sensor, every drone, and every operator into one coordinated network. Not just for India. For every nation that believes sovereign defense infrastructure is non-negotiable.

// WHY NOW

The Anduril moment
has arrived for India.

Ukraine proved that the future of warfare is attritable, software-driven, and networked. Low-cost autonomous systems coordinating at the edge defeated expensive isolated platforms. India watched. India's borders face the same threat vector — and the indigenous response does not yet exist.

iDEX, MQ-9B procurement, the push for Atmanirbhar defense — the policy environment has never been more aligned. The window to build India's Anduril is open right now.
